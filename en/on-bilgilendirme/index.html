Allergy information

If you have a known allergy to dyes, polish, care products or essential oils, tell us when you book.

Patch test

For hair colour and some care products, a small patch test at least 48 hours before the service is recommended.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, tell your specialist beforehand; we choose products and massage accordingly.

Sensitive or irritated skin

We do not treat areas with wounds, irritation or skin sensitivity; we may ask you to reschedule.

Age limit

Guests under 16 need parental consent and must be accompanied.

Cancellations and changes

Please let us know at least 24 hours ahead if you need to change or cancel an appointment.
